This document records the primary external market research used to shape the **GOODFORM** 40-SKU catalogue, collection hierarchy, label-transparency rules, and source-backed popularity badges.

GOODFORM never fabricates sales figures, bestseller ranks, customer star ratings, review counts, countdown scarcity, clinical outcomes, or third-party certifications. Every "Category Signal" badge displayed in the storefront traces directly to one or more of the four cited sources below, with methodology caveats preserved intact.

## 2. Amazon India Shelf Estimates & Methodology (`impuls8`, Nov 2026)

### Headline Shelf Size
- **Estimated floor:** **>= INR 991.3 crore** in annualised supplement sales on Amazon India (**excluding protein**, which sits on an adjacent INR 210.3 crore shelf).
- **Breadth:** 70 search queries across 3 pages each yielded 7,485 distinct listings; 3,135 listings displayed monthly purchase badges, and 2,426 were admitted under Amazon's supplement categories across **435 brands** (~1,377,000 units/month). Without brand-named queries, the category-only floor is INR 867.2 crore.
- **Ownership mix:** Indian D2C brands capture **41.1%** of tracked shelf value (and acquired D2C brands another 9.0%), ahead of legacy Ayurveda houses (9.5%) and multinationals (7.7%).

### Critical Methodology Limitations (Must Always Be Stated)
- **Lower-bound badge arithmetic, not audited GMV:** Estimates multiply Amazon India's "bought in past month" badge lower bound by observed page price times 12.
- **Seasonality bias:** Data was read on **4-5 November 2026 (Diwali week)**. Promotional traffic and gifting during Diwali can elevate monthly purchase badges relative to a full 12-month average.
- **Badge truncation:** Listings without a monthly badge contribute zero to the floor.
- **Buyer literacy signal:** On Indian Reddit threads (505-comment magnesium comparison), buyers actively audit whether magnesium glycinate labels disclose true ~14% elemental magnesium by weight vs undeclared magnesium oxide buffering. GOODFORM therefore prints both **compound weight** and **elemental nutrient amount** on every mineral SKU.

## 4. Loop Health India Workforce Health Index 2026 (n = 7,592 Valid Answers)

- **Urban Indian workforce adoption:** 34.1% of working professionals named at least one supplement from an 8-item prompt (Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, multivitamins, fish oil/omega-3, magnesium, creatine, prebiotics/probiotics, collagen); 65.9% selected none.
- **Leading nutrients in urban India:** **Vitamin D (20.4%)** and **Vitamin B12 (15.0%)** led all categories; no other option exceeded 13%.
- **Separate laboratory context (unlinked sample):** In Loop's separate laboratory dataset, 60.1% of 6,943 Vitamin D panels were below 20 ng/mL, and 69.6% of 6,192 Vitamin B12 panels were below 300 pg/mL. Because survey and lab samples are unlinked, GOODFORM cites these strictly as educational background in `/learn` and never as diagnostic claims.
- **Demographic patterns:** Women reported higher overall use than men (42.6% vs 30.3%), rising with age, whereas Creatine skewed younger (5.1% in ages 20-29 vs 0.9% in ages 50-59). City adoption ranged from 42.4% in Delhi NCR to 29.4% in Chennai, with Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Kolkata, and Hyderabad in between.

## 6. How GOODFORM Uses These Signals Honestly

1. **Source-backed "Popular in Category" tags only:** Product cards and detail pages may display a discrete `Market Signal` note citing the external category source (for example, *"Magnesium: #2 in ConsumerLab 2026 survey; INR 86.3cr Amazon IN category floor"*).
2. **Zero synthetic social proof:** GOODFORM renders **0 star ratings**, **0 fabricated customer reviews**, **0 "X bought today" counters**, and **0 fake strikethrough urgency timers**.
3. **Compliance overrides popularity:** Even when an ingredient shows market volume (Shilajit INR 37.0cr, Milk Thistle INR 22.2cr, Ashwagandha INR 19.0cr, NAC #20 ConsumerLab), GOODFORM keeps those SKUs **unpublished** until India-specific FSSAI legal, botanical, or non-specified ingredient review is completed.
